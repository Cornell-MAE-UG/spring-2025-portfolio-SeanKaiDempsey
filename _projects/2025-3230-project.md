### Technical Insights
This project involved a complete engineering teardown of a commercial humidifier to analyze its fluid systems, piping losses, and internal aerodynamics. While the full breakdown, testing process, and results are detailed in our video report below, my core engineering contributions focused on two primary areas:

- **Pipe and Heater Flow Analysis:** I derived the fluid velocity profiles through the internal piping geometries using fundamental mass conservation and head loss hand calculations. This allowed us to map pressure drops across the heating element and evaluate internal flow efficiency.
- **Turbomachinery Characterization:** Our team investigated the internal blower fan profile. By taking precise geometric measurements of the impeller, we identified it as a **forward-curved centrifugal fan**. I used these dimensions to construct fluid velocity triangles, verifying how the blade curvature explicitly optimizes kinetic energy transfer to pressure head relative to the system's volumetric flow targets.
